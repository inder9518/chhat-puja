About Our Chhath Puja

Gurugram Chhath Puja ek aisi parampara hai jahan hum har saal milkar Chhathi Maiya aur Surya Devta ka aashirvad prapt karte hain. Yeh parv hum sabko ekta aur shraddha ka sandesh deta hai.

Har saal kai bhakt aur parivar is pavitra utsav mein bhaag lete hain, jahan hum bhajan, aarti, aur prasadam ke madhyam se apni bhakti pradarshit karte hain. Aapka swagat hai humare parivar mein!
